Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Calculate Clearly is a static educational website. It has no user accounts, no comment system, and no checkout, and it does not ask visitors for personal information in order to read its guides.

Information we collect

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that only to reply and to maintain a record of corrections and correspondence.

Automatic information

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may record standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and operation of the service. The interactive calculators on this site run entirely in your browser: the numbers you enter are not transmitted to us or stored.

Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Third-party services embedded or linked from this site operate under their own privacy policies.

How we use information

  • To respond to messages and corrections.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which guides are useful, so we can improve them.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

External links

Guides link to external calculators, including on toolshorizon.com. When you follow an external link, that site's privacy policy applies.

Your choices

You may ask us to delete email correspondence you have sent us by writing to the address on our Contact page. Server logs held by the hosting provider are controlled by that provider's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
