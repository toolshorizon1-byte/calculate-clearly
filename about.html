About Calculate Clearly

Calculate Clearly is a small, independent educational publishing project. Its single job is to explain — clearly, accurately, and without sales pressure — the calculations ordinary people meet in money, school, health, and daily life.

Who publishes this site

This site is published by the same publisher as Tools Horizon, a website of free online calculators. We state that connection openly: when a guide links to a Tools Horizon calculator, it is because the calculator performs the calculation the guide explains — and the link is labelled with the site's name so readers always know where they are going. This is not a third-party review site, and nothing here should be read as an outside endorsement.

Editorial standards

  • Every guide shows its formula and at least one worked example.
  • Assumptions and limitations are stated in the article, not hidden in footnotes.
  • We do not invent statistics, testimonials, reviews, or expert credentials.
  • Health, tax, and finance guides carry clear “education, not advice” notices.
  • Articles are reviewed when the underlying rules or ranges change.

Author

Guides are written and reviewed by the Calculate Clearly Editorial Team, the publishing team behind this project. We use a team identity rather than invented individual experts, because we will not claim personal qualifications we have not evidenced.

What this site is not

It is not a financial adviser, tax preparer, or medical service. Use the guides to understand a calculation; use a qualified professional for decisions about your own money, taxes, or health.
